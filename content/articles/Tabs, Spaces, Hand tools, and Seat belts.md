# 8/14/26
https://newsletter.powderworks.dev/p/tabs-spaces-hand-tools-and-seat-belts

I read this today and mostly hated it. The world the author describes just kinda sucks and makes me feel bad. 

I think some of the things they offer up are misguided. The analogies to seat belts and woodworkers in particular. (Which is kinda the whole point of the article).

But, I found it to be well written. I found the outlining of the phenomenon of the industrialization of software engineering to be very informative. It is a particularly useful lens.

In particular, it helped me understand AI as a tool _intended_ to deskill workers. Its not an accidental harmful side effect, it is in fact a desirable property of the tool from management's perspective.
