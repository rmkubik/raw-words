# 3/3/26
That sounds like a pitch for a quirky indie rpg, but its not.

I see some thinking around note-taking like [[Evergreen notes]] that discourage tying time to notes. I'm not sure if they'd agree to that statement or not, but headings with dates are certainly not encouraged. You're supposed to make a perfect atomic note that is always going to be relevant.

I think my context as a take a note (or really feel any feeling at all) is super important. I'm going to think a movie sucks if I've already seen 3 like it, or I slept poorly, or somebody was rude to me on the bus.

I think dating my notes and looking back at them in the future, reacting to that note is interesting. Often, I find myself embarrassed or smiling at what that naive person thought weeks or years ago.
