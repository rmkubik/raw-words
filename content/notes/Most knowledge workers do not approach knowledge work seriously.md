We're never really how to do knowledge work in school. We figure out some things that work for us adhoc while getting our jobs done. We mostly just stick with that and don't investigate it much.

Things like [[Evergreen notes]] and [[How to Take Smart Notes]] investigate alternatives. Especially the idea of [[Evergreen notes as a fundamental unit of knowledge work]].

This leads to most note taking being ineffective [[Most people take only transient notes]].
