## living
I'm living in Portland, OR. 

I've been getting a bit into gardening lately. Trimming and pruning and weeding and mulching and irrigating. I still mostly have no idea what I'm doing and hoping I don't kill anything, but it is nice to have some rewarding physical tasks to keep up on.

I bought a bike earlier this year. It has been a blast this summer!

My main "project" this year has been [[Being a Guy Who Does things in 2026]]. It's going really well. I've been having BBQs, hanging with friends, doing things outside, grabbing coffee. This has been a real healthy shift in my perspective from wishing to have community to making it happen.

## making
I finished organizing [Portland Festival of Gaming (PFOG)](https://pdxmakes.games/pfog) earlier this year and I wrote [[A PFOG 2026 Retrospective]] with my thoughts afterward. Summary is – it went great! I want to do more! 

I've been helping organize some more, smaller events around computers and games and play. Coffee chats and powerpoint parties and park days and barbecues. I think "community" is most of what I'm making right now. That's all going on [here](https://pdxmakes.games/) and it has been really rewarding.

I've been feeling more up to working on personal projects lately. Mostly a few game projects lately. I'm not really successfully finishing anything at the moment and that bothers me some times.

I'm trying to write about more honest things on this site. I've tended to publish more "safe" posts. I don't think this gets me what I want to get from writing.
