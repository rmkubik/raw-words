If something calls to you in the moment to take a note on, do it. Otherwise, let it go.

If it is relevant to you it will come back up again. 

It is easy to get bogged down trying to catalogue everything interesting thing that you come across.

This is a good way to [[Getting lost in your knowledge management system is a fantastic way to avoid creating things]] and not actually have creative output.

I suffer from this explosion of interesting links that I feel like I should record constantly... I'm not 
super sure how to apply this one in practice for me. It's hard to remember: [[You will NEVER be able to take every note]].
