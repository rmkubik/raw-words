]This is a core theme of the slipbox system described in [[How to Take Smart Notes]].

I have also observed this in my own thoughts and experiments.

This is also why [[I think that trying to maintain streaks is bad for you]]. If you fail a streak (and you will) you're disincentivized to do that thing again. Instead, you should have a system that celebrates your return.

[[Your personal system should never force you to work on what you don't want to do in the moment]]. 

[[How to Take Smart Notes]] also references the importance of motivation for successfully completing more work than rigidly sticking to a plan.
