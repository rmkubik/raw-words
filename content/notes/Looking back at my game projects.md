# 10/12/26
I looked through a whole bunch of old files yesterday and feel like this year has been a bit of a stagnation on personal projects. I think there are several reasons there:
- There are about 5-10 game projects or other project with screenshots/recordings as I flipped through my old files per year. That's not actually a number that feels very fast when you're living it, but when you look through them all it can feel more impressive.
- Many of those projects didn't lead anywhere, just like my current projects. Many of those projects were much much smaller prototypes, false starts, or throw away ideas. Many of my current projects are much larger in scope and more ambitious.
- It seems like a lot of my project work is actually concentrated in the winter seasons. Both the first part of the year and the end of the year. This makes sense that creativity in my life would be more seasonal like that.
- Many projects I looked through had collaborators. I'm almost never collaborating now. I'm not even participating in jams or other community oriented experiences that would give me a reason to work on things. I think Decker Day is the only real counterpoint I can think of to this from this year.
- Some of my BIG PROJECTS from olden years I do think are very different from my current situation. I do think these are some casualties of my current disillusionment and burn out. Looking back at TwilioQuest and Wildfire Swap and Island Maker and Terminal Escape and Caved. I think those projects were kind of the peak of my creative games output.
	- TwilioQuest was done for full time work. I had to build that one.
	- Caved was a contract that I also had to deliver.
	- Wildfire Swap was my first big project that I had engaged with Drew and Wes so I had community inspiration to push that one through.
	- Island Maker was part of a community jam. I think this one is still a fluke in a way. It was very creatively engaging and I picked a perfect scope level and iteration process.
	- Terminal Escape is a similar idea of just having a great scoped game for a game jam.
- Aside from some large projects that I had either financial or social obligations to work on, I have not finished any games outside of game jams.
