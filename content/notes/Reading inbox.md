Have a reading inbox - this helps you [[Close open loops]].

Make a zero friction way to capture interesting links without them plaguing you forever. The tricky thing to avoid is having these inboxes become infinite growing lists.

An ideal [[Reading inbox]] should encourage you to cull stale items. If items don't call to you, you should prune them. You don't need to catalogue them. [[Your natural salience filter is a great determinant of what’s most alive to you]].
