Because you're using [[Evergreen notes]] to publish [[Writing a piece becomes an editing process if you build on top of evergreen notes]], it can be tempting to edit notes for an audience other than yourself.

This is not ideal because it slows down your note taking process. It can cause you to un-usefully be [[Getting lost in your knowledge management system is a fantastic way to avoid creating things]].

[[Writing and editing are two different tasks]]. Trying to both at the same time makes each one worse and harder to do.
