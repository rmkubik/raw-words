This is a core pitch of [[How to Take Smart Notes]] and [[Evergreen notes]].

If you do your knowledge work as a web of evergreen notes, then areas where you have a natural concentration of notes will have enough material to write a piece about.

Look for clusters in your notes, you can likely assemble these notes into a larger piece. At this point, you already have your first draft when you sit down to write.

Now you can just edit this series of thoughts into a publishable piece.

This builds on [[Knowledge work should accrete over time]].

If you [[Create speculative outlines while you write]]  and link evergreen notes as you write them, you can start to slowly build up pieces you want to write about over time. This gives structure to the accretion.
