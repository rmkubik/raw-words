# 7/9/26
I'm feeling very interested in "simple" strategy games that do not need to be infinitely played roguelikes.

Simple is not what I mean, but just games that don't need to be replayed infinitely. Its fine if they become solved at a certain point. As long as they do so in a thoughtful way.

[[Inscryption]]'s core card game is actually very inspiring to me here. It is decidedly NOT simple, but like it's whole design is AROUND obsoleting itself.
