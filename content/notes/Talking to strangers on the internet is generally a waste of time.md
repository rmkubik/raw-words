This is maybe more of a [[Social Media]] inspired thought. But anywhere communication is done with strangers seems to mostly devolve into conversations I get no value from. Worse, these conversations are often addictively infuriating. So, not only are they worthless but they also consume more of my time than they should.

I think there is a lot of value in learning from strangers, but chatting about it online seems to usually suck. Having a one on one conversation can be really enlightening. Reading a well thought article or essay from a stranger is great.

There are online communication channels dedicated to actually having a valuable conversation, but those are rare and require effort to maintain.

Most social media channels seem to devolve into a competition. Someone is always trying to win the conversation. These conversations give mostly no value.

This also has me reflecting on [[The medium is the message]] for Social media. [[Thoughts on social media]] does a sort of critique of Social media through this lens, but I didn't feel like it was quite correct to me. The author concludes it is still largely good, where I feel differently.
