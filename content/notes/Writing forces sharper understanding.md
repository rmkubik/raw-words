You don't really understand things you *think* you understand until you're forced to be explicit about that understanding. Shipping something publicly is great for this, but takes a lot of time. Just writing an [[Evergreen notes]] about something is usually a good first step to get clarity.

When you start writing about an idea or thought you have you will quickly realize it was much more a fleeting and poorly thought out construct.
