# 3/4/26
How do I get more comfortable with uncertainty? I was thinking about how [[For good and bad, I cannot tell who reads my blog]]. Before I ever had a phone, you could just set up a time and place to meet someone and then go meet them. You didn't need to text to confirm. 

I think I want to be okay not knowing if anyone is reading my blog posts. I do think I want to intentionally share them with others as an act of communication. I think the point of posting is to communicate with others. Well, _a_ point at least. That's why I'm posting these notes instead of just leaving them private on my own computer.
